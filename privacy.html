Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we do

Nimbo turns photos of notices into editable text.

Your images and text

In standard mode, text is extracted inside your browser and your image is not uploaded. If you use Accurate mode, Explain or Translate, the image or text is sent through our server to an AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the result. We do not store it on our server.

Saved notices

Saved notices are kept only on your own device (browser storage). Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Analytics

With your consent we use Google Analytics to count visits, countries and devices. You can decline in the banner.

Payments

Payments are handled by Paystack. We never see or store your card details. Your email is shared with Paystack to process the payment and send a receipt.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you may ask what data we hold or request its deletion. Contact: bigebenezer55@gmail.com
